# Assessment-2                                                                                                                                                                                # Assessment 02 — Auralis Nova Premium Product Advertisement

## Project Overview

This project presents a premium advertising key visual for a fictional high-end wireless headphone brand called **Auralis**.

The flagship product featured in the campaign is **Auralis Nova**. 

The objective was to create a sophisticated, photorealistic product advertisement inspired by high-end global consumer electronics campaigns.

The visual focuses on:

- Premium product presentation
- Strong product hierarchy
- Sophisticated industrial design
- Cinematic lighting
- Realistic materials
- Clean advertising composition
- Negative space for future copy and branding

---

## Tools Used

- Leonardo AI


---

## Creative Direction

The creative direction was based on a premium technology advertising aesthetic.

The headphones were designed with:

- Precision-machined metal details
- Matte and satin surfaces
- Soft-touch ear cushions
- Brushed-metal accents
- Minimal industrial design
- Refined product geometry

The overall visual language was kept sophisticated and minimal rather than using excessive futuristic effects.

---

## Composition

The final composition follows a landscape 16:9 advertising format.

The headphone is positioned slightly to the right of center.

This creates generous negative space on the left side for potential:

- Advertising copy
- Product messaging

The product remains the dominant visual element.

---

## Environment

A dark premium studio environment was created using:

- Deep charcoal tones
- Graphite surfaces
- Subtle architectural forms
- Atmospheric depth
- Controlled shadows
- Soft reflections

The environment was intentionally kept minimal so that the product remains the primary focus.

---

## Lighting

The lighting direction was designed to communicate premium craftsmanship and advanced technology.

The visual uses:

- Controlled volumetric lighting
- Soft rim lighting
- Subtle highlights
- Realistic specular reflections
- Controlled shadows
- Cinematic contrast

The lighting was kept restrained to avoid an artificial or overly neon appearance.

---

## Camera & Photography Style

The image was designed to resemble professional commercial product photography.

Visual characteristics include:

- Three-quarter product angle
- Controlled perspective
- Long-lens commercial photography look
- Shallow but realistic depth of field
- Crisp product details
- Physically believable reflections
- High dynamic range

---

## Image Specifications

- Format: Landscape
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9
- Minimum Target Resolution: 720px
- Product: Auralis Nova
- Visual Type: Premium Advertising Key Visual

---

## AI Generation Approach

The generation process focused on maintaining a consistent product concept and premium advertising aesthetic.

Prompts were developed around:

- Product design
- Material quality
- Lighting
- Camera composition
- Environment
- Negative space
- Photorealism

Multiple generations were reviewed and refined to achieve the final visual direction.

---

## Output

The final output is a single premium advertising key visual featuring the Auralis Nova wireless headphones.

The image is designed to be suitable as a campaign hero visual or product advertising artwork.

---

## Quality Evaluation

The final visual was evaluated based on:

- Product visibility
- Product dominance
- Realistic materials
- Lighting quality
- Composition
- Negative space
- Photorealism
- Clean geometry
- Advertising suitability

---

## Known Limitations

Generative AI can introduce small variations in product geometry or material details between generations.

Potential limitations include:

- Minor geometry variations
- Small surface-detail inconsistencies
- Slight variations in reflections
- AI-generated product details that may not represent a physically manufactured product

---

## Conclusion

This assessment demonstrates the application of generative AI for creating a premium commercial product visual with controlled art direction, cinematic lighting, realistic materials, and advertising-focused composition.
